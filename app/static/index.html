How to photograph leaves

1. Plain matte white paper

Place leaves on a clean A4 or Letter matte white sheet or flatbed scanner glass. Avoid textured cloth, wood, or glossy surfaces.

2. Press leaves flat & separated

Flatten curled leaves (under clear non-glare glass if needed) and leave a small gap between leaves so blades and petioles do not overlap.

3. One 2.00 cm calibration square

Place the printed 2.00 cm square flat in the frame on the same plane as the leaves. Use only one calibration square per photo, and keep the other sheet's square out of the frame.

4. Camera straight above (nadir view)

Hold the camera directly overhead (within 15° of vertical) in diffuse, even lighting. Save at full resolution (at least 800 × 800 px).
